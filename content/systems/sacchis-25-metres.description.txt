Sacchi's 25 Metres 🧠⚽️

Back four on the halfway line, strikers 25 metres ahead, never more. Force the pass out to the full-back, arrive with three men and the touchline, and concede the far side on purpose. Then the line steps 8 metres on one call, and the ball over the top is already behind him.

Offside is not a defence. It is an attack.
