I started a football channel in May to test one idea: that you could build an audience without using a single second of footage.

No clips, no highlights, nothing borrowed. Every frame animated from scratch in code, on a pitch drawn to real geometry.

88 videos later, it is at around 5 million views and 25,000 followers.

The surprise was not the reach. It was what people message about. Almost never the tactics. Almost always the videos themselves. What is it made in. How long does one take. How do you decide what happens in the first three seconds.

So I am opening the whole thing up.

The first half is live now. Every breakdown the channel has made has its own page, written out phase by phase, the diagram beside an explanation of what is happening in it. Free, no account: totalfootball.naurra.ai

The second half is the process. The engine every video is built on. The choreography. The pacing rules, which are not taste: they came out of measuring all 88 published pieces and finding that watch-through behaves like a gate rather than a gradient. Below the threshold, nothing we made ever broke out. Not once in 43 attempts.

That becomes a course.

If you make content and you have ever wondered whether the careful, analytical version of your niche can beat the loud version, our data says yes, and not narrowly.
