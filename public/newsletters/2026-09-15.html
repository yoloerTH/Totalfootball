Studio update · 15 September

The ball leaves the ground.

Until this week every pass in the Studio stayed on the grass, even the ones that should have been in the air. Not any more. Switch is now Loft, and it finally does what it always looked like it did: draw one and the ball climbs off the grass, grows as it rises, and leaves its shadow on the spot it came from.

Select the arrow and a Height slider sets how far off the floor it goes: low for a ball pulled back across the box, high for a goal kick. Every switch you have already drawn is a loft now and flies on its own, so there is nothing to redo.

While we were there, we fixed the shadow too. It used to turn with the ball on every pass and gave it a faint wobble the whole way. Now it stays put under the ball, and stays down on the grass when the ball goes up.

 

Also new this week

Empty grass for your drills. Y-drills and passing circuits don't fit in a rectangle. On a Training grid you can now switch off the borders and markings and get a blank patch of grass. Everything stays to scale, so 10 metres is still 10 metres. You just mark the area out yourself. Look for Empty grass under Area settings.

Drag phases into order. No more clicking a phase along one spot at a time. Drag any thumbnail in the phase strip and drop it where it belongs. A gold line shows where it will land.

Vertical videos show the whole drill. Portrait exports were cutting off the sides of wide training boards. On a 50 by 45 metre grid you got 31 metres of the width. Now you get all 50, and Gentle, Standard and Close each frame the shot differently again. We also fixed the dark bands that showed up across the grass in some exports.

A better first visit. New coaches now see their first steps as soon as they open the Studio, so if you invite your staff, they can find their way around without you.

Coming soon

The Studio in your pocket.

Last week we said the iPhone and iPad app was close. It is now with Apple. Here is what you will get when it lands.

The same account, the same boards. Sign in with the account you already use here and every system, sequence and folder you have made is already on your phone. Change something on the touchline and it is waiting on your laptop when you get home.

The whole Studio, not a viewer. Build a shape, set the next phase and press play. Your squad, kit and crest come with you, and you can send a link, a still, a sheet or the film itself straight from the app.

Made for both. Portrait on the phone, landscape when you turn it, and the full board on iPad for team talks. We will email you the day it is on the App Store.
