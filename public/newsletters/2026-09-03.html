Studio update · 03 September

A community of coaches.

Until now, the Total Football Studio has been your private workspace. A place to design systems, capture sequences, and collaborate with your own staff. But football is a conversation, and the best ideas come from sharing them.

We are building a social layer directly into the Studio. Soon, you will be able to connect with other coaches worldwide, share your system ideas, and discuss tactics in the open. As your ideas gain traction, you'll earn followers and unlock status badges to showcase your tactical reputation within the community.
