The problem

France had the pace.
Spain removed the runway.

The principle

Defend the picture before you have to defend the player.

The proof

The sequence is simple when it is viewed from the ball outwards.

01
Keep the ball.

02

03
Arrive as two.
Mbappé did not lose a one-against-one. He ran into a cage of green shirts before he could turn.
