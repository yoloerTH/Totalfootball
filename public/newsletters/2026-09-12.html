Studio update · 12 September

Get ready for mobile.

We are finalizing the last details of the Total Football iOS app, and it will be available on the App Store very soon. Built specifically for iPhone and iPad, it will allow you to take your systems, tactics, and sessions straight to the pitch.

You will soon be able to review your plays in the locker room, present tactics on the sidelines, and sketch out ideas anywhere inspiration strikes. We can't wait to get it into your hands.

Stay Tuned
 

New in the Studio: Collaborate on official systems.

While you wait for the iOS app, we have added a highly requested feature to the web Studio. You can now interact directly with our official systems. This is the first step toward a more collaborative and social experience we have been planning behind the scenes.

You can now leave comments and share your perspective on any official system. Even better, you can hit the "Suggest a variation" button to open the system in the editor, make your tactical adjustments, and seamlessly post your variation as a comment for other coaches to see.

To keep the discussion professional and accountable, you just need to set your coaching handle (@) and name in your profile before posting. Dive in and start sharing your ideas!

 

We can't wait to share more with you soon.
